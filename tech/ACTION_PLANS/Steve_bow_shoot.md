# Action plan 4b-Steve: bow shooting rules (branch claude/steve-bow-shoot, RESTART, one new action number; Sal's items are the other plan sal-bow-items)
Driver: design/PLAYABILITY_PREP.md row 4b (build 795). Today: `_throw` flies a thrown weapon (THROW_SPEED, THROW_GRAVITY 9.8, lands, never hits an animal), `_strike` jabs an animal at 2-3 m through the `hit_animal` hook, `server_animals.damage(id, amount, source_peer, kind)` is the one damage hook (hurt animals bolt, bleed, leave a blood trail). There is no bow, no arrow, no flight that hits anything.
1. Goal: hold to draw, release to shoot: an arrow flies on an arc, hits the first animal on its path or the ground, wounds or kills, and can be picked up again.
2. Protocol: ONE new action, ACT_SHOOT = 49 (check free at build time): a = slot of the bow, b = draw time in ms (client-measured, server clamps to draw_max_ms and checks the pace), at = aim direction. Same hold pattern as the shore net (ACT_NET), no other message, no protocol bump beyond the action number (PROTOCOL.md note).
3. Server rules (new src/stream/server_bow.gd, called from server_items `action`): bow = item with weapon_class "bow"; ammo = the first item with ammo_class "arrow" in the pack (a quiver holds more; Sal's item). Draw below draw_min_ms: a weak shot (25 percent power) with a line of text. Power = lerp over draw time; speed = power x speed_max (about 45 m/s full draw), gravity 9.8, range cap 80 m. Each 0.05 s step of the flight tests the segment against animals through a new hook `hit_animal_path(peer, from, to, damage, kind)` (sphere radius per species in animals.json "hit_radius_m", default by size) and against the ground; first thing hit wins. Damage = arrow damage x power x skill factor "bow" (skills.json action, practice on a hit) -> `server_animals.damage(id, amount, peer, "arrow")`; a hurt animal then bleeds and bolts as today. Shot noise: animals within noise_m (40) of the shooter are alerted through the existing senses block (a bolt, not a kill). Arrows: after landing in ground, break_chance per tip (stone 0.35, flint 0.25, wood 0.5, bone 0.3); an arrow that hit an animal drops at the animal and survives half as often; water: lost 50 percent (design). Survivors become world items with wear, picked up like any item. Everything random goes through fate()/roll_chance so luck applies.
4. Data: new data/items/bow.json (draw_min_ms, draw_max_ms, speed_max, range_m, pace_ms, noise_m, weak_power, break_chance by tip, water_loss, ammo ids list; every number there, none in code); arrow damage and tip live on Sal's arrow items (damage, tip). Missing ammo items are skipped.
5. Client: minimal hold-to-draw input beside the net's (left hold with a bow in hand; release sends ACT_SHOOT; a small draw bar in the existing hold UI). Looks of the bow and arrow are Kathleen's and Sal's; the flight is drawn by the existing thrown-item path with the arrow model.
6. Tests (headless): test_bow.gd with stub animals and ground: full draw hits a stub deer at 20 m and damages it; a short draw falls short and lands; the arrow stops at the ground and is picked up again; break chance counts match the data over 1000 shots; no ammo gives the message and no shot; pace limit; server clamps a forged draw time; water loss. Plus local_check 233, parse_scan; David3 runs them.
7. Out of scope: arrows into water at fish (plan G), headshots, wolves' reaction beyond the existing alert, crossbows, the bow looks.
Risk: medium (new action, new flight); additive, nothing existing changes. Merge after sal-bow-items (needs the ammo item ids).
