# Action plan 4c-Steve: trap rules (branch claude/steve-hide-traps, RESTART; Sal's snare and hide items are the other plan)
Driver: design/PLAYABILITY_PREP.md row 4c (build 796), EARLY_GAME_LIST 17 ("hide, hide clothing, traps"). Today: hide and hide clothing items exist (Sal), carcasses give parts through carcass.json, animals wear game trails (data/animals/trails.json, server_trails.gd, a strength per 4 m cell), nothing catches an animal while the player is away.
1. Goal: a snare the player sets on an animal path catches small game while they do something else; they come back, find the catch, and work it with the knife like any carcass (meat, hide).
2. Item side (Sal, own plan): item `snare` (placeable, twine + thorny canes or sticks, weight, durability), looks by Kathleen. This plan only needs its id `snare`, structure kind `snare_trap`, and a placeable struct entry in structures.json with needs-ground-contact rules like other placeables.
3. Server rules (new src/stream/server_traps.gd, called from server_items' tick; one struct per snare in `_items` with struct kind snare_trap, saved like other structures): state "set" -> "sprung" with a catch species, or "empty/broken". Every check_s (30 real seconds, only for snares near a player or loaded, cheap): the chance this check = base_chance x trail factor (worn game-trail strength at the snare, 0 to 1, so a snare on a path catches, one in open grass rarely) x animal factor (species within lure_m of the snare, from the animals module: rabbit, fox, duck; deer and bigger never: snare_max_size) x bait (optional food on the ground within 1 m doubles it, consumed) x weather (storm x0.5) x night (x1.3: small game moves at dusk and night, Dave's hunters know it). A catch picks the species by the animal module's nearest eligible record, removes that animal from the world (it is in the snare), turns the struct into a carcass of that species lying at the snare (reusing make_carcass and the knife flow, so butchering, hide, skill practice and rot already work) and wears the snare 1 (durability). Catch number goes through fate()/roll_chance so luck applies. A snare that sat sprung for longer than rot time loses its catch with the carcass rot.
4. Rules the player sees: set with a click on ground (placeable), empty snare shows a loop; a sprung one shows the animal; Esc/Tab nothing new. Limit snares_per_player 6 (the oldest is taken back, the item returns). Cannot set within 8 m of a structure or on water. A snare picked up returns with its wear. Animals avoid a snare they have escaped from for 10 game minutes (the same rabbit is not caught twice in a row).
5. Data: data/animals/traps.json: base_chance, check_s, lure_m, snare_max_size, species weights (rabbit 1.0, fox 0.4, duck near water 0.5), trail_factor curve, bait_mult, night_mult, per_player cap, avoid_s, wear per catch. No numbers in code.
6. Protocol: none new. A snare is a placeable struct sent like any struct; the catch is a carcass struct. PROTOCOL.md note only. Old clients draw both as plain blocks.
7. Tests (headless, test_traps.gd, the animals and trails modules with stub tick): a snare on a trail catches rabbits at the data rate over 2000 checks (stat band), a snare in open grass catches under a fifth of that, deer-size animals are never caught, a catch becomes a carcass that works with the knife and gives the usual parts, the snare wears and breaks at its durability, the per-player cap returns the oldest, no catch while no animal is near, saves and reloads a set snare. Plus local_check, parse_scan; David3 runs them.
8. Out of scope: baited cage traps, pit traps, trapping big game, taming, snares looks, selling pelts. Order: needs Sal's snare item and hide item first (merge after them).
Risk: medium-low; additive (new module, new struct kind). CHANGE_LABEL RESTART.
