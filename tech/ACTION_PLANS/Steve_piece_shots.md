# Action plan: piece shots (branch claude/steve-piece-shots)

Problem (Mike/Kathleen): the look rig's `--pieces` row (shot_runner.gd `_show_pieces`) frames from eye height at 4 m, so low pieces (bed, floor, hearth, snare) come out as 60 px slivers and nobody can judge the look. Held items already have the 4-view orbit (item_cam.gd `views()`, fit by the piece's AABB).
Change (RUNTIME, debug tooling only, no game rule, no protocol):
1. New runner mode `--shot-piece=<structures.json key>[,<key>...]` (tools/look_rig.sh passes it like --pieces): for each named piece, build the model with the game's own `client.items.ghost_model(state)` (same builder as `_show_pieces`), stand it alone on flat ground ahead of the player, then take the 4 views of the item job (front, side, back, top-down/3/4) from `ItemCam.views(aabb, fov, ground_y, aspect)`.
2. Framing: fit distance from `ItemCam.fit_distance` clamped to 3-6 m (MAX_FIT_DIST raised to 6.0 for pieces only, passed as a parameter, items keep 3.0); the piece fills at least a third of the frame (MIN_FILL) and every corner stays inside 90 % (FIT_EXTENT), the same pass/fail the item job prints ("shot runner: piece-view N ok/FAIL fill x%"). Big pieces (lean_to, thatch roof) end at 6 m with a wider orbit; a piece taller than wide gets pitch 10 degrees, a flat one (bed, floor, snare, hearth) pitch -25 degrees so the top shows.
3. Names tag (Label3D) and the piece's size line stay as in `_show_pieces`; a piece that draws nothing says FAIL; a `planned` piece is skipped with a line. Snare: if structures.json has no snare_trap yet (Sal's branch not merged) it prints "skipped: not in structures.json" instead of failing the run.
4. Tests: test_item_cam.gd gets piece cases (flat bed AABB 1.0x0.3x2.0, tall wall 4x3x0.3, lean_to 4x3x4: fit distance in 3..6, fill >= 1/3, all corners inside); parse_scan; David3 runs `--shot-piece=rough_bed,thatch_floor,clay_hearth,oven,lean_to,snare` on the box and I read the pictures. Kathleen reviews.
Risk: low (debug-only code path; the normal game is untouched). Merge order: any time after build 817.
