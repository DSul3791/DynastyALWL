## coffer-heir | Banking: when a character dies, what happens to their coffers?
- They pass to the heir
- They pass to the Dynasty head
- They stay locked until a will opens them

## coffer-atm | Banking: is there a fee to use coin from a faraway coffer for a big deal?
- Free
- Small fee (a few percent)
- No remote use: the deal must be sealed at the coffer

## deal-offline | Banking: can a big deal be sealed while the payer is offline?
- Yes, the agreement waits and seals when the coin is there
- No, both sides must be online
