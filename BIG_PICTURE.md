# Dynasty — big picture

Orientation: [DOC_MAP](docs/DOC_MAP.md) is the main design index; [CLAUDE](CLAUDE.md) routes current operation. [Art Direction](art/Art_Direction.md) and the [architecture budget](ARCHITECTURE_BUDGET.md) own detailed appearance/hardware targets.

**The game.** DynastyALWL, "Dynasty, a life well lived": a persistent, rough-medieval, multiplayer online world with a "Sims vibe". Survival, exploration, town building, family, heirs and a dynasty that outlives one character. Near-photoreal look (Dave's brook photo wins); sound counts as much as content and visuals. Runs on weak hardware (1050-class, 8 GB, 30 fps minimum).

**Principles (DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md).** Safe, then kind, then generous. Do it yourself or hire it out (no job only by hand, none only by NPC). Nobody is forced to build a town. Content, visuals and audio count equally. Server, client and admin tool stay separable.

## Historical project snapshot

Legacy scope (snapshot date unverified): one starter town on tile (3,4) plus eight neighbours for a friend session. This is historical, not a current content census or freeze.

**Features reported then, current status unverified:** Generated terrain and water simulation, flora, walking/swimming/sleeping, items and crafting, campfire, foundations and walls, test NPCs (Hob, Edda), tunnel watch (off).

Obsolete branch/freeze, staffing, queues and QA/daily-reading instructions are superseded; the [source overview at 21be054](https://github.com/DSul3791/DynastyALWL/blob/21be0543772f67b1c94be88ed3a91f59918d91ed/BIG_PICTURE.md) preserves them.
