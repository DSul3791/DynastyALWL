## 1. The job, where it starts and stops
I own the RULES and CODE of the game's sub-systems, mostly in `src/stream/server_items.gd` (9993 lines, about 820 functions) and the client scripts that carry them:
- Character system: server records (`_characters`, `characters.json`), the client character store and select screen, learned recipes and starter goals per character, idle timeout, first-minutes smoke tests.
- Comfort and the Rested buff; food and drink (hunger, thirst, cooking rules, eating, drinking); warmth and cold; sleep and energy.
- Lit fires and fixtures (lit or unlit, fuel, who can light, saved lit state); placement rules for kits and structures; death and respawn rules (`data/items/death.json`); goal and hint triggers; stump and trunk work.

**Not mine:** how anything looks (Kathleen, Kathreen: fire LOOKS, light node values, creator preview, flame models); item, recipe, drop and hint TEXT and data (Sal; I read his keys and tell him which key I need); plants and animals (MikeW); terrain and water mechanics (Sonny); the first-hour playthrough (Dino); the test suite, rigs, probes and merge gates (Tyler; I write the one small test per piece of logic, he runs the full passes). I never merge to main or the daily; the moderator merges my branch `claude/steve-systems-4680f3`.

## 6. How to tell it is finished
- Two characters on one machine have separate inventories, recipes, goals, Rested and spawn bed; deleting a character removes its server record; tested.
- Save-compat: an old file loads, a new field gets a default, a corrupt record does not lose the others; tested.
- The first-minutes smoke test and the select screen test pass headless; no `items tick slow`, `STALL` or `WATCHDOG` line.
- Mutation checks catch deliberate breakage of comfort, goals, lights, hints and novice grace.
- The playability bot and Dino's step list pass with fire, shelter, bed, Rested, cooking and eating working, and placement refusals each give a reason.
- Real death is built to Dave's decision, or he says it is out of scope.
- Every system in section 2 has a "state" and no unexplained "not reviewed".
