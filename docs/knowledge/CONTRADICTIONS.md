## C. Checked and agreeing

- **Lapse rate:** 6.5 °C per km on every page and in the code.
- **Rain that puts a torch out:** 0.2 rain strength with a 5 s grace (torches and firelight pages, `flame_rules.gd`).
- **Fresh snow** stops hushing sound and stops drifting after about 2 days (sound and snow-on-slopes pages).
- **Frost:** freezing spray, frozen wet sand and frost heave all start at an air temperature of −2 °C (sea and soils pages). Salt water freezes at −1.8 °C (sea page and `still_water_ice.md`).
- **The calm-night quiet:** a background of 18 to 26 dB (sound page), with 22 dB used for hearing ranges (#683).
- **Torch burn:** the game's 30 minutes is inside the measured 19 to 61 minutes for wooden torches (firelight page).
- **Treeline:** `tile_rules.json` puts it at 620 m (glaciers page). **Our sums:** under the decided 9 °C ±5 climate, the warmest month's mean of 14 °C at sea level falls to 10 °C at about 615 m. A warmest-month mean of about 10 °C is the usual treeline marker (general knowledge, not checked today). Under the old 10 °C ±14 weather (before #620) it would have sat near 2 km.
