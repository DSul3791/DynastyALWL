# Items test walkthrough (builds 418 to 441), about 20 minutes

Before you start: pull, close the editor, and check the HUD build number says 441. Stand near the starter town. Press ] once or twice for stone, sticks, plant fibre and logs. Nothing here needs a re-bake. Write down anything that looks or feels wrong; I have not seen any of it by eye.

## 1. Crafting and the camp bench (3 min)
1. Open the inventory (I). Craft cordage from plant fibre, then a Camp bench (12 sticks, 3 cordage).
2. Hold it (hotbar number). You should see a small plank in your hand and a ghost of the bench. Left click to place it.
3. Try to craft a Stone spade. You need a wooden spade, 2 stone and cordage. Stand away from the bench: the recipe is greyed with "needs camp bench" and the queue refuses. Walk to it: it works. Start a craft and walk off: it should wait, then finish when you come back.
4. Craft a Tool rack and a Wooden vice and place both within 5 m of the bench. The Backpack recipe says it needs level 2 until the rack (and vice) stand next to the bench.

## 2. Clothes and warmth (3 min)
1. Craft a Grass cloak (16 fibre, 1 cordage), Grass skirt, Fibre sandals. In the inventory press Use (E) on each. Tab shows a Wearing section with Take off buttons; the Warmth bar should hold up better in the cold.
2. Cold is mostly a winter and arctic matter. The temperature now swings with the time of day (cold just after dawn, warm mid-afternoon).
3. The hide set and backpack need hides, which do not exist yet.

## 3. Fire, food and cooking (3 min)
1. Place a campfire kit. Hold E near it to roast berries or nuts. Eat the roast (Use): your Warmth bar rises a little. Hover raw food: raw meat, fiddleheads, beans, acorns and wild mushrooms warn they make you sick.
2. Eat one raw acorn, if you can find one: you should be sick for about 6 game hours (a label on the inventory and Tab screens, sometimes a thought line). Sleeping or tea helps.
3. Teas: stomach tea, chamomile tea and willow bark tea cure sickness faster. They keep 8 game days.

## 4. Fishing (4 min)
1. Craft a Fishing rod (2 sticks, 2 cordage). Walk to a pond or the river and hold it.
2. Left click at the water: "You cast your line". Wait 6 to 22 seconds. When it says "Something bites! Click now." click again within 3 seconds. You get a trout, perch, carp or pike. Berries in the pack are used as bait automatically.
3. Cast again and again: the pond's hidden stock runs down (a small pond holds 4 to 12 fish) and refills one fish per game day, which is one real hour. Walk away 6 m and the cast is cancelled.
4. Spear: hold a spear, stand still a moment at the water's edge and jab at the water. About one jab in five lands a fish for a new character.
5. Ice (winter only, or use the clock): on a bearing sheet, right click with an axe 6 times to cut a hole; then cast the rod from beside it.

## 5. Death and remains (3 min)
1. Put a few items in your pack. Swim far out into deep water and stay under for over 60 seconds (the breath bubble runs out).
2. You die, fade, and wake at the spawn with an empty pack. Open Tab: "Your remains" lists distance, direction and time left. Walk back: remains look like a dark body with a pack. Hold E to search them and take your things. Only you can open them for the first day.
3. If you die more than 5 times, the oldest remains crumble with a warning.

## 6. Skills and inspection (2 min)
1. Tab shows your skills; chop, dig, mine (also by breaking a boulder with an axe), craft and fish a few times and watch the bars creep up (slowly; the speed is a guess).
2. Press / for inspection mode and look at your bench, your remains, or a kit lying on the ground: each is named plainly.

## What to report back
Anything that looks wrong or is confusing: the bench in the hand, the greyed recipes, cast and bite timing (6 to 22 s, 3 s window), how fast sickness feels, whether remains are easy to find.
