# Dynasty — Complete Tutorial, Guide, Goals & Coin Rewards

**Status: FULL-GAME WALKTHROUGH DESIGN DRAFT FOR CREATOR REVIEW — NO GAMEPLAY IMPLEMENTATION APPROVED YET.** Revised 13 November 2026. This is not a short onboarding tutorial: it is the continuous instructional progression and reward system spanning first materials, tools, fire sizes, workstations, construction, production, weapons and the highest currently implemented items.

## What each goal must teach

Every **Goal** table cell contains both its player-visible title (before the em dash) and instructional **How-to** text (after the em dash). The second and only other column is the automatic monetary **Reward**. The Journal should display the title in the goal list and the How-to in the selected goal details; the HUD shows only the first two unfinished goal titles, never the long instructions.
Every How-to must truthfully identify where materials come from, exact recipe ingredients and quantities, correct tools, required station and its level, and the gameplay action to perform. A station must exist before the player is asked to operate it; intermediate products must be obtainable first. When the live source only establishes a recipe but not a resource's acquisition method, mark the unresolved source in this review draft and do not pretend it is an approved player instruction.

**Goal completion and coins are entirely automatic, server-authoritative and once per character.** No acceptance, activation, pinning, claiming, or new crafting locks; any valid earlier/out-of-order completion must earn the goal even if it is not shown in the HUD. Press J for Journal Index, G for Goals and M for Map. The ornate Goal Achieved placard uses the blank art in `C:\Users\david\Downloads\GoalAchieved` and a trumpet fanfare, while the upper-right HUD is a thin bordered box headed Goals with precisely the two earliest unfinished titles. Every Admin/Debug action requires Ctrl+Shift+its key.

## Verified milestone requirements and design review issues

- **Twine:** harvest plant fibre from nettles/tall grass; 3 plant fibre make 1 Twine by hand via I. The inherited completion goal currently requests 2 Twine.
- **Campfires:** recipe 6 Stick + 3 Stone makes the campfire kit; place, add fuel (F), and ignite (E). As proven by `data/items/cooking.json` and `src/stream/server_items.gd`, 3 firewood pieces yield a medium fire and 3 logs yield a bonfire; a hanging pot caps the tier and damp wood can delay it. Medium and bonfire tier goals require dedicated real server completion signals.
- **Wood route:** fell a tree, cut a loose log into wooden blocks with an axe; Wooden - Hammer requires 1 wooden block, 1 stick and 1 twine. The Chopping Block can split blocks into firewood. **Discrepancy requiring audit:** the `chopping_hard_firewood` recipe instead lists an oak log; do not teach them as the exact same action until reconciled.
- **Metal and boards:** Smelter must exist for iron ingots; Smithy requires 3 iron ingots; Iron - Saw requires 2 iron ingots and 2 sticks at Smithy. The saw makes Planks (6 per softwood log) or Hardwood Boards (4 per hardwood log) by hand before the advanced Sawmill exists. The Sawmill requires Planks, nails, a water wheel and iron ingots. Do not present the Sawmill as the only way to produce its own required Planks.
- **Higher tiers:** Brick → Kiln → Lime powder; Mixer + sand/stone/water → Concrete; Blast furnace + iron → Steel ingots; Iron/steel tools and metal hammer; Master smithy; Engineered construction; Fortified construction. Verify each intermediate recipe and station/add-on in play.
- **Important:** The progression order below is not an in-game unlock gate. The first table is a guided main route; subsequent tables cover the remaining available craft/build achievements, including alternate activities, so the first two HUD goals stay useful. This **ordering approach is a design proposal** pending creator confirmation; it does not modify the automatic completion rules.
- **Not complete until tested:** The source-aware dependency sort is a static check, not an AI playthrough. Every recipe variant, station level, material source, door/roof variant and all unlisted gather-only/loot/animal/crop items must be audited before this catalog can be marked approved.
