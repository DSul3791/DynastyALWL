## 2. Principles of the plan

1. **Data first.** Every phase starts by extending `data/` tables and docs. Code follows only where a new *shared system* is needed.
2. **Nothing blocks on the rebuild that does not have to.** Phases 0 to 3 use only what the current terrain can answer. Water creatures and anything place-bound to the new water wait for the record (R6).
3. **One creature at a time, with a clear completion criterion** (Dave's rule for MikeW, applied to every phase): done means it works in the game, not that a test passed.
4. **Small PRs, one owner.** Each phase is split into PRs an owner can finish in a day. Leftovers go to the owning named agent.
5. **No cloud testing.** Checks marked *PC* run on Dave's PC only, scheduled around the rebuild runs.
6. **Each phase ends with something Dave can see or play.**

### Phase 1. The creature record: schema, cards and the matrix *(docs and data only)*

- **Goal.** Every creature the game has is described the same way, and we can see the gaps.
- **Deliverables.**
  - **M-03.** The extended habitat keys (`altitude_m`, `slope_deg`, `rock`, `water_kind`, `cave`, `canopy`, `sea_depth_m`, `sky`, `lair`, `season`) added to the *schema doc* and read as optional by `animals.json` loaders. No placement uses the new keys until a system can answer them.
  - **M-17.** An interaction card for each existing species (about 20) and `docs/design/INTERACTION_MAP.md`, the creature × verb matrix, generated by a small script from `animals.json` and the cards.
  - A gap list: habitats with no Discover creature, tiers with no Avoid creature.
- **Done when.** The matrix exists and the gap list is the input to Phases 2 to 6.
- **Owners.** Morgan (cards, schema doc), MikeW (reads the keys), Jamie (doc map and knowledge entry).
- **Dependencies.** None. Safe to run now. It does not touch MikeW's model work.
- **Risk.** Schema churn. Mitigation: all new keys optional, none required.

### Phase 2. Discovery without new creatures *(client UI, existing animals)*

- **Goal.** A player can *learn* the animals already in the game.
- **Deliverables.**
  - **M-18.** A field journal page with the knowledge ladder (Heard of, Sighted, Observed, Tracked, Studied) for existing species. Observation and sighting are detected from the senses the animals already use; Studied from a carcass or a taming.
  - `animal_sign.json` coverage: at least two sign kinds per species, shown in the world and in the journal.
  - **M-07.** Crows and ravens as a sign: gather near a kill or carcass. Reuses the carcass system.
- **Done when.** Dave can play the starter island, see a sign, follow it to a fox or a deer, and see the journal fill.
- **Owners.** Steve (journal and knowledge state, the per-character data), Kathleen (journal look), MikeW (sign), Jason (calls).
- **Dependencies.** Ordering with Steve's loading work; the journal is character data and must survive heirs (M-18, inherited).
- **Risk.** Scope creep into a full UI. Mitigation: a plain list first, art after.

### Phase 3. Island archetypes and rumours *(design plus world sketch; no new creatures)*

- **Goal.** The world map says what each island is *for*, and players can learn about it.
- **Deliverables.**
  - **M-01.** An archetype table in `world.json` (or the world plan file) as a tag per island, derived from its feature (arc, hotspot, fragment, ridge, atoll). The sketch already knows the origin; this adds the label and the intended habitat set.
  - **M-19.** Rumours on the news rail: a rumour is a (fact, place, time) from the world, voiced by the model, stored by the game. A first set of ten rumours for the starter ring.
  - **M-08.** Bat emergence at dusk at inhabited caves (needs only the cave and the clock).
  - A written *content brief per archetype*: biomes, creatures (owned and needed), places, hazards, rewards.
- **Done when.** Dave can read one page per archetype and the world plan carries the tag.
- **Owners.** Morgan (briefs), Steve (news rail), Sonny (the sketch tag, after the rebuild's current step), Jason and Kathleen (bats).
- **Dependencies.** The world sketch is stable. **Not** dependent on R-steps.
- **Risk.** Conflict with the rebuild's changes to the sketch inputs. Mitigation: tags only, no change to geometry.

### Phase 5. Peaks, water and fishing *(needs the rebuild's record)*

- **Goal.** The glacial lakes, falls and wetlands that Sonny's rebuild creates become living places.
- **Deliverables.**
  - **M-06.** Fishing (rod, spear, net), the fish rows, rivers that carry fish by discharge and temperature, and the autumn salmon run on rivers that reach the sea.
  - **M-20/M-21.** Eyries on crags and hidden tarns, placed by the rebuild's slope, rock and cirque tags.
  - **Climbing.** A climbing verb and kit. This is a **new mechanic** and needs a Dave-approved design first: *Recommended:* hand-holds on steep rock, rope and pitons as items, stamina cost.
  - **M-12.** Marsh lights on fens and bogs.
  - Wetland creatures by type (marsh, swamp, fen, bog).
- **Done when.** Dave can climb to an eyrie and watch an eagle, and fish a river whose size matches its flow.
- **Dependencies.** Rebuild R3 (lakes), R4 (wetlands), R5 (network), R6 (record), R7 (runtime). Phase 4's soaring and taming. **A hard dependency:** do not place water creatures before R6.
- **Owners.** MikeW, Steve, Sonny, Kathleen, Jason.

### Phase 6. Coast, sea and the hot row

- **Goal.** Sailing is an adventure with life in the water and on the shore.
- **Deliverables.**
  - **M-11.** Whale and dolphin pods as sailing encounters; albatross as a navigation clue; sharks at blood; tide pools; the sea monster as a rare encounter in dangerous waters.
  - **M-24/M-25.** Sea stack colonies; wrecks and sunken ruins with life.
  - **M-02.** The hot-row animals: camel, lion, leopard, hyena, rhinoceros, warthog, wildebeest, elephant, kangaroo (if Dave wants it), placed on the dune and savanna island archetype.

- **Done when.** A sailor crosses open water, sees a whale, lands on an atoll and meets a turtle.
- **Dependencies.** Ships and sailing (from the economy and building design), ocean water look (`docs/water/ocean_m5.md`), the world sketch tags (Phase 3), the creature pipeline's batch 2 and 3, models for sea life (**Need**).
- **Owners.** MikeW (rows), Kathleen (looks), Jason (sea sounds), Sonny (ocean), Steve (ship movement).

### Phase 7. Fantasy creatures and the hard islands

- **Goal.** The mythical layer, built with the five-point test (M-16).
- **Deliverables.**
  - A fantasy roster, a handful of creatures each with a real base, one exaggeration, a niche, a warning and three verbs. Candidate list: antler-moss stag, stone-hide boar, ember-wolf, cave bear (scale variant), roc, yeti, sandworm, troll, hippogriff-like mount, kraken, wyvern.
  - **M-10.** Lair puzzles (the troll in a cave, the roc on a peak).
  - Regional variants of existing species by material and scale (frost wolf on ice islands, ember boar on fire islands) so one model serves many places.
  - Fire, ice and storm hazards for the extremes.
- **Done when.** Each creature passes the five-point test and has an interaction card.
- **Dependencies.** Models: Dave makes creatures in Meshy as he did the goblins (committed, because he made them). Taming (Phase 4) for mounts. Soaring (Phase 4) for the roc and the wyvern.
- **Owners.** MikeW (rows), Kathleen/Kathreen (looks), Dave (Meshy models), Jason (calls).

### Phase 8. The ultimate island and the abyss

- **Goal.** The centre of the world, as Dave designed it.
- **Deliverables.** Dragons and wyverns, sandworms and scorpions, the fallen city with ruins, labyrinths and boss lairs, remains with gear, the lava river, the old road, the greatest loot (`DESIGN_IDEAS.md` 4.13); the trench's deep-sea floor caverns; M-14 and M-26 to M-28 as authored places.
- **Done when.** A prepared expedition crosses the abyss, lands, survives a day and brings something back.

- **Owners.** All.

## 7. How a creature gets added (the recipe)

So it stays a row of data, in this order.

1. **Idea card** approved by Dave ([Dave] tag).
2. **Interaction card** (GUIDING_DOCUMENT section 5).
3. **Five-point test** if fantasy (M-16).
4. **Model**: owned, or made by Dave. If bought, never committed.
5. **Rows**: `animals.json` (movement, habitat keys, temperament, seasons, young, tame), `models.json`, carcass and parts, seasons, sign, sound slot, `KIND_OF_SPECIES` (the donkey's recipe in `ANIMALS_BATCHES.md`).
6. **Check on the PC** (Dino): it appears, faces the right way, plays its clips, behaves as the card says.
7. **Knowledge**: a line in `docs/knowledge/animal-behaviour.md` for anything learned.
8. **Matrix** regenerated; gaps rechecked.
