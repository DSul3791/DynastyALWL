# Dave's rulings

One live line per subject. The line here beats any older text anywhere: designs, data notes, code comments, tests and Claude's own picks. Quotes are Dave's words; lines without quotes are a short summary of his ruling. Older approved specifications still live in their topic docs (`DOC_MAP.md`) where nothing here replaces them. Coordinator and thread picks are not rulings and do not go here. A part marked *(unverified: coordinator's note)* comes from the coordinator's notes of Dave's words, not his own words yet; it still holds.

**Rule, suggestion or unclear.** Dave, 26 Nov 2026: "Only explicit rules where I say don't do something or lock something in are rules. everything else are suggestions" (R-CORE-RULES-VS-SUGGESTIONS). Each line says which it is after its date. The marks are the threads' and coordinator's reading of his words, not his own: a **rule** is a line where he forbids something or locks something in ("should" alone is not); a **suggestion** is everything else: build it as the starting point, and when play-testing shows something better, make the game correct (R-CORE-PLAYTEST-WINS), change the line and its check by the procedure below and report the finding to the coordinator. **Unclear** marks a line whose words are mixed or not his own yet (unverified); it counts as a rule until the coordinator settles it. The coordinator settled the first 22 unclear lines on 26 Nov 2026 by the coordinator's reading of his test, and kept R-TEAM-LESSONS (ruling changes come to Dave first) as a rule; those marks are the coordinator's reading. Coordinator's reading, not Dave's words: a rule that play-testing shows wrong is not changed by a thread; the finding goes to the coordinator, who asks Dave. A rule's mark is changed only with a new dated line in his words. A "don't" that only describes how a real animal behaves (R-ANIMAL-BEAR) is not a rule. The checks block on rules and unclear lines and print a NOTE on suggestions; a check on a line whose text says unverified only warns, whatever its mark.

**Recording a new ruling** (`ai-team/CONSTITUTION.md`, "Rulings"): in the same PR, (1) find the line here on the same subject and replace it, never add a second; mark it rule, suggestion or unclear; (2) search the repo for the old rule (`git grep -i`) and change or delete every active line that still states it (designs, data notes, comments, tests, the bug list); (3) add the old rule's telltale phrase to `retired_rules.json`; (4) add or change the check that guards it in `tools/check_dave_rules.py` (plain numbers from the data, or the Godot test that covers it); (5) run `python3 tools/check_rulings.py` and `python3 tools/check_dave_rules.py`. Git history keeps the old wording; no "superseded" notes in the docs.

## Core
- **R-CORE-ROADMAP** · 25 Nov 2026 · rule · Order of work: the physical world right; a full crafting and survival play-through; NPCs living and working; only then the Dynasty layer. Looks-only work waits. *(unverified: coordinator's note, date recorded)*

- **R-CORE-CAVE-RESCUE** · 23 Nov 2026 · suggestion · "if you are in a cave and end up getting rescued they should deposit you at the nearest civilization and for these purposes that means campfire for now."
- **R-CORE-NO-GATEKEEPING** · 23 Nov 2026 · rule · "we are not gate keeping by limiting resources."
- **R-CORE-NOT-SPAWN** · 23 Nov 2026 · rule · Nothing in the world is based on where the spawn is (river rise after rain follows the rain, not the starter area). *(unverified: coordinator's note)*
- **R-CORE-RULES-VS-SUGGESTIONS** · 26 Nov 2026 · rule · "Only explicit rules where I say don't do something or lock something in are rules. everything else are suggestions"
- **R-CORE-PLAYTEST-WINS** · 26 Nov 2026 · rule · "make it correct according to playtesting not according my old rules. its ok if playtesting finds that my rules were not right."
- **R-CORE-NEWER-WINS** · 23 Nov 2026 · rule · "newer rules override older ideas." 25 Nov: "we need a system that removes old contradictory rules when new rules are established" (the procedure above).
- **R-CORE-FOCUS** · 23 Nov 2026 · suggestion · "I want all of your focus on making the game incredibly smooth and comfortable." Basics first: movement, weights and fighting animals before extras like snow.
- **R-CORE-NIGHT** · 25 Nov 2026 · rule · "I do not agree with a shorter night. I think that’s because they’re spoiled with old video games. I think the night is big part of this game they just haven’t caught on yet."
