## Defect found and fixed
**A blueprint site served as its station.** Step 3, first run: the Loom blueprint went down as a site (materials short), and Fibre cloth was still woven. Minimal reproduction (run on this PC): an empty pack plus a wooden hammer, `ACT_PLACE_BLUEPRINT` the Loom (a site is set down), `station_near(1, "loom")` returns true, and 8 plant fibre queued as `fibre_cloth` gives 1 Fibre cloth. The same held for any station (a Smithy or Blast furnace site, a Food preparation table site) and for addons (a Quenching trough site enabled Tempered; an addon site raised a station's level).
- Approved design: `docs/BLUEPRINT_SITES.md` (Dave 5 Nov 2026): "Sites count nothing as built (no goal event, no cover, no support) until finished."
- Fix (small, within that design): `serving_station`, `_cold_station_near`, `_addon_kinds` and `_addon_beside` skip structures with `struct.site` (4 lines). Every station lookup goes through these (`station_near`, `_craft_store`, `_wheel_slow`, the crafting reach step, `craft_variants`).
- Test: step 3f. Positive control run: with the fix removed (`git checkout` of `server_items.gd`), the walkthrough fails only at "3f. a Loom blueprint site with its materials still to come weaves nothing"; with it, it passes.

No other defect was found. Things the first runs tripped on and that are **not** defects: the craft queue takes at most 20 crafts per entry (`CRAFT_QUEUE_MAX_COUNT`); station products wait in the station's store (T18); the 30-slot pack fills; a bog deposit runs out (60 ore, regrows); the vitals tick settles the body weight back to its range (so the lifted carry limit had to be set again); the cart's `load_reach_m` is 3 m.
