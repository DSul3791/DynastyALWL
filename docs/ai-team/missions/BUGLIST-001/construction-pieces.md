## BUG-011

Reproduction (by reading): `thatch_roof` (state 43) was a 2.4 m square gable on four posts (`piece_look.thatch_roof`, a ridge, two slabs, posts 2.5 m tall), `piece_kind: roof`: it snapped only to a foundation's five roof anchors or edge to edge to another roof (`placement_rules.snap`, `_join`), never to a wall top, and was always flat at the foundation top. So it had legs and no way to rise or fall from a wall.

Design (documented here; no design document covered it, the report's Required line is the spec):
- A roof panel is 2.0 m wide (a wall's length and a ceiling cell) over a 2.0 m horizontal run, slab 0.16 m thick, `slope_deg` 45 or 65: rise 2.0 m or 4.289 m (`run_m * tan(slope)`). Spot (stored position) = the panel's middle; it rises toward its local -Z, i.e. the way the player faced. A 4 m house therefore takes two panels meeting at a ridge.
- Connections (`PlacementRules.roof_connections`): every 2 m cell of a wall's top (any tier or opening; walls with `height_m` honoured), a ceiling's (piece_kind `floor`) four edges at its top, and both ends of a standing roof panel. Foundation edges are not connections (a wall stands there and the roof meets the wall top).
- Either end anchors (`roof_panel_spot`): mode 0 anchors the LOW end and the panel goes on past the edge and rises; mode 1 anchors the HIGH end and the panel comes back the other way and falls from the edge. The facing (quantized to quarter turns) is the rise direction and must lie across the edge; looking along an edge gives no snap (red ghost "face across it"). Mode is flipped with the mouse wheel while the roof blueprint is picked (notice: "Roof panel rests on its low end and rises from the edge").
- Idempotent: the ghost sends its snapped middle and yaw and the server snaps again; an edge counts by the nearer of its own distance and its panel's middle's distance to the spot, so an answer snaps to itself (tested, including two walls 2 m apart).
- Refusals, ghost and server alike: nothing to rest on ("nopanel"), the low edge sinking into the ground ("buried", `roof_panel_buried`, ground sampled at the low edge's middle and both ends, 0.1 m tolerance), a second panel with the same middle ("close", `one_to_a_point`). A panel is never placed free on the ground.
- Files: `src/shared/placement_rules.gd` (new `ROOF_PANEL_KIND`, `roof_panel_rise`, `roof_connections`, `roof_panel_spot`, `roof_panel_buried`, `snap(..., mode)`, reasons), `src/stream/server_items.gd` (`_place`: mode from `lift_steps` clamped 0..1, refusals, the 1.5 m "ground under it" test skipped for panels as for wall tops), `src/client/held_item.gd` (ghost: mode, `nopanel`, `buried`, wheel, `lift_steps` reset when a panel is picked or dropped), `src/client/piece_look.gd` (`thatch_roof(slope_deg)`: one tilted slab of straw, three rafters under it, six eave bundles along the low edge; ONE tilted collider slab, `_solid` gained an optional rotation), `src/client/look_table.gd` (`thatch_roof` args 45, new `thatch_roof_65` args 65), `src/shared/cover.gd` (`cover_below_m` so the rain box reaches the floor under a panel on a wall top), data.
- Data: structures.json `thatch_roof` (piece_kind `roof_panel`, `slope_deg` 45, `run_m` 2.0, footprint 2 x 2, cover box half 1 x 1, `height` 0 = to the panel's middle, `cover_below_m` 3.9) and new `thatch_roof_65` (state 291, `cover_below_m` 5.1); blueprints.json (rename, new 251, `next_number` 252); items.json (rename 263 kit and 264 piece, new 1255, `next_number` 1256 in items.json and gathered.json); encyclopedia.json (both names, new row, "Used in" lines of twine, thatch and stick); docs: `DYNASTY_MASTER_ITEMS.md`, `catalog_ledger.json` (new entry under catalog "BUGLIST-001", state IMPLEMENTED - UNVERIFIED), `progression.md`, `art/BUILDING_TIERS_LOOKS.md`.
- `Crude - Roof corner` (`thatch_roof_corner`) is untouched: it has its own builder (`roof_corner("thatch")`), not the straight roof's, and keeps its four posts. Question for Dave: the corner is still a post-supported hip piece, which no longer matches the posts-free Roof 45 / 65.

Saved placements: ids and numbers are kept (state 43, kit, item 264, blueprint 31), nothing is deleted. A roof placed before this change (position = the foundation top, a gable above it) reloads as a 45 degree panel centred at that old spot, i.e. low and probably inside its floor; the player takes it down (refunds its materials) and rebuilds it. No migration was written: no save-format change is allowed and Dave ruled no old-save migration is needed (new game).

Tests added or changed (all NOT RUN): new `src/worldgen/tools/test_roof_panels.gd` (+ `.uid`): data (names, ids, numbers, states, unique), both slopes x both facings x both anchored ends on a wall top (anchored end equals the wall top's middle, middle half a run and half a rise away, the answer snaps to itself), looking along the edge, two walls, ceiling edge, a chain (65 on the 45's high end) and a ridge, the second-on-same-spot "close", buried and not buried, a real server (nothing to rest on, rise, second refused, 65 fall refused, 45 fall stands, chain, save and reload, `_kind_now`), the cover box (under, beside, on top), the look (mesh in the panel box with no posts, exactly one collider, solid through the slab and to both ends, tilted the right way) for both looks.
