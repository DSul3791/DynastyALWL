## BUG-008 Crude - Foundation red "too steep" on apparently suitable ground
- Reproduction: by reading code only (not run). The ghost (held_item.gd `_update_blueprint`) sampled the five ground points with `_camera.global_rotation.y`, the camera's free turn. The foundation is placed at the quarter turn snap() returns, and the server (server_items.gd `_place`) samples at that snapped yaw. A 4 x 4 m square turned 45 degrees reaches 2.83 m from its middle instead of 2.0 m, so on a 0.14 slope the ghost read a 0.79 m rise (red) where the server reads 0.56 m (accepts); the answer changed as the camera turned.
- Second cause (fact, same function): the ghost's ground probe was a bare physics ray of every collider, so a tree, bush, placed piece or the player standing inside the footprint counted as ground; the server reads terrain height only.
- Root cause: evidence for both (placement_rules.gd `steep`, held_item.gd probe, server_items.gd steep call). That Dave's red was one of these two is a likely guess, not observed.
- Change: held_item.gd `_update_blueprint` passes `deg_to_rad(_blueprint_yaw)` (the placed turn, already used for the water check) and a new `_terrain_y` probe that looks through players, plants and placed pieces (`CaveSounds.is_not_rock`, the repo's existing "not rock" test) and returns the terrain. Snapped (beside/stacked) foundations never ran the slope test (their height comes from the neighbour); they are covered by the ground-reach check below.
- Tests added (NOT RUN): src/worldgen/tools/test_foundation_wall_placement.gd `_slope_checks`: level, moderate (0.4 m), just inside (0.5992), just over (0.6008), steep (2 m) on the server and in `Rules.steep`; the 0.14-slope quarter-turn versus camera-45 case; a foundation asked at 45 degrees is taken; a run of foundations downhill.
- Regression risks: the ghost's ground probe still depends on the client collision mesh matching the server's generated height (as before). `placed_structure`/`species_ids` meta is not on every placed body, so some odd props may still read as ground.
- Test coverage, plainly: the BUG-008 fix (ghost yaw and terrain probe) is client-only in held_item.gd and has NO automated test; it is covered by the F6 player check only. The 45-degree assertions in test_foundation_wall_placement.gd pass on dev as well (they document the geometry and that the server takes the quarter turn); the slope cases test the shared rule and server, not the ghost's sampling.
- Status: NEEDS PLAYER VERIFICATION. F6: hold a Crude - Foundation, aim at gentle ground (about 8 degrees or less) and turn the camera all the way round: the ghost stays green at every turn; on a clearly steep hillside it is red with "the ground is too steep here". If red still appears on suitable ground, capture the footprint samples, slope, threshold (0.6 m) and the reason shown (BUGLIST proof line): a third cause is not excluded.

## BUG-009 Green Crude - Wall at a foundation corner refused "You cannot place that there"
- Reproduction: by reading code only. That exact sentence has one source: the server_items.gd `_place` ground-height check. For a piece on a foundation (`on_base`) it required the terrain under the wall to be within 1.5 m of the wall's height. A wall at an anchor stands at foundation y + 0.3. With the wheel's lift (up to 1.0 m) on a slope the foundation passes its own 0.6 m rise check, yet the downhill anchors lie 1.54 m under the wall (0.12 slope, lift 1.0) and were refused: the two anchors on the one downhill side, with a green ghost, because the ghost had no such check. A wall on a stacked foundation (layer 2 top 2.3 m up) was refused at every anchor.
- Not the cause: occupancy. Pieces never block pieces (`both_pieces` on the server, `blocked()` on the client), so adjacent blueprints and walls do not count; the occupancy refusal says "Something is in the way", not this sentence.
- Root cause: the server's ground check applied to pieces whose height comes from a standing foundation, and the ghost did not run it (preview != commit). Evidence: server_items.gd `_place` (was line 3734), placement_rules.gd `snap` (wall anchor y), held_item.gd (no ground check). That Dave's foundation was lifted or stacked or part of a downhill run is the inference that fits "two of four corners"; not observed.
- Change: one shared rule `PlacementRules.off_ground` (+ reason "float": "it is too far above or below the ground"), used by the server and by the ghost. A piece standing on a foundation (wall, floor, roof, pillar, railing, wall top, fire) is exempt, the foundation having passed its own check; a foundation on the ground keeps 1.0 m, beside another 1.5 m, stacked +2 m per layer (unchanged numbers). The server refusal now says why: "You cannot place that there: it is too far above or below the ground." The ghost goes red with the same words. The server's wall-top special case is subsumed (deleted).
- Files: src/shared/placement_rules.gd, src/stream/server_items.gd, src/client/held_item.gd, tests below. 2 m / 4 m wall compatibility and double doorway: untouched (snap/span_anchors unchanged).
- Tests added (NOT RUN): test_foundation_wall_placement.gd `_off_ground_checks`, `_wall_corner_checks`: a foundation on a 0.12 slope lifted 1 m takes a Crude - Wall at all eight anchors (the downhill ones fail on dev) beside built walls and blueprint sites (every second neighbour is converted to a site), a downhill run of foundations (third refused naming the ground), and the same walls on a stacked foundation; wall height equals foundation top. test_placement_notices.gd updated to the new sentence. Save/load: no placement row format changed, so no save case was added.
- OPEN (not fixed) occupancy gap: the ghost's `blocked()` knows only fires and states 5/6/7 (lean-to, chest, chopping block; `inventory_view.placed_rows` adds a few more for fires), while the server counts any non-exempt structure within 2.5 m (ladder, well, bed, stairs, kiln and others), so a wall near one can be green and then refused "Something is in the way." (different message; not this report).
- OPEN (not fixed) ghost leniencies versus the server: (1) a boulder under a footprint still reads as ground in the ghost's probe (no placed-structure meta on boulders' StaticBody beyond `boulder`); (2) terrain more than the probe depth below the ghost (6 m for slope, 14 m for the float check) reads NAN and is skipped, so the ghost stays green where the server may refuse; (3) furniture (a non-piece kit) set on a foundation is not snapped to it (snap returns on_base false), so it keeps the 1.0 m ground rule and is refused "too far above or below the ground" on a foundation lifted or stacked higher than that, on the server and in the ghost alike (red, truthful, but not placeable there); (4) a Serving Platter on a table on a foundation: the server snaps with foundation rows only, so it is checked against the 1.0 m ground rule and refused there; the ghost now does the same (no exemption for a table base), it is not made placeable.
- Status: NEEDS PLAYER VERIFICATION. F6: lift a Crude - Foundation 1 m (mouse wheel) on a slope, then place Crude - Walls on all eight edge spots: each green ghost places (blue blueprint) and none says "cannot place that there"; a floating spot shows red with "too far above or below the ground".
