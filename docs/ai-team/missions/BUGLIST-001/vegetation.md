## BUG-006 Distant grass appears flat, then drops/aligns onto slopes (Medium)

**Reproduction:** not reproduced (no Godot in cloud). Traced by reading code; the mechanism below matches Dave's description exactly.

**Where the height comes from (the three things the brief asked to separate):**

1. Height sampling. Ground grass is not placed from terrain data (trees are: flora_placer.gd uses `data.height_at`). `src/client/grass_view.gd _build` casts one downward ray per sample point against the terrain's own collision (`intersect_ray`, ~line 1740) and every card of that sample point is written at that one hit height (`buffer[o + 7] = point.y - 0.03`, was ~line 1898). Samples per 6 m cell by distance band (`_lod_points`, radius > 50 m): 4x4, 3x3, 2x2, 1 (bands near/mid/far/very far; near_end 14-20 m, mid_end 0.5 r, far_end 0.8 r). Cards are scattered `+-0.5 * spacing * 0.95` round the hit (x and z random), so in the very far band a card can be 2.9 m from the ray that gave its height, in the far band 1.4 m, near 0.7 m. FACT (code). **ROOT CAUSE A:** on a slope of normal y 0.8 a card 2.9 m uphill of its hit floats about 2 m above the ground (or sinks the same on the downhill side); the cell reads as a horizontal terrace over the hill (the "flat" look), and each band change rebuilds the cell with denser samples, so it re-aligns ("drops onto the slope") as the player approaches. Inference from code and geometry; the screenshots were not available to me.
2. Mesh/data sync. Collision comes from VoxelLodTerrain at `collision_lod_count` 4 (LOD 0-3, `terrain_client.gd:1133`); `lod_distance` is not set, so the module default applies (48 m for LOD 0, doubling - from my memory of the module, UNVERIFIED on the PC). Grass beyond that is placed on LOD 1/2 collision (2/4 m voxels). Existing `_check_ground` (Dave 4 Nov, floating rocks over a cave) re-casts 5 kept probes only in the 48 s after the cell was built (`CHECK_TIMES`). A cell that is built far away and waits longer than that is never re-checked when the fine ground arrives; it is only rebuilt when it crosses into band 0 (14-20 m), so the correction is a late, single jump. **ROOT CAUSE B (contributing, smaller on smooth ground: below the 0.35 m threshold nothing moves):** the check ladder is keyed to build time, not to the arrival of finer ground. FACT (code); the size of the LOD 1/2 vs LOD 0 difference on real hills is a guess.
3. Placement. Nothing is wrong with the terrain seed or worldgen; none is touched. Trees/flora use analytic data and are not affected.

**Change (src/client/grass_view.gd):**
- `lie_y(point, normal, x, z)` (static): each card's base y is taken on the plane through its ground hit with the hit's surface normal, not at the hit's height. No extra rays; the normal is already in the hit and `normal.y >= MIN_SLOPE_Y` (0.58) is guaranteed by the `continue` above. Applied to the grass layers (cover/short/tall). Litter, pebbles, shells, wrack, reeds, boulders (`_put_litter`, `_put_shore`) still use the hit height: they are drawn only within litter_m/shore_m of the player (near band, 1.5 m spacing, +-0.7 m), so not the far-flat symptom. Left alone (scope).
- Re-check on arrival of fine ground: when the player first comes within `FINE_GROUND_M` (64 m) of a built cell, its probes are armed on a short ladder `FINE_CHECK_TIMES` [2 s, 8 s] (reusing `_check_ground`; rebuild where ground moved > 0.35 m, in place, same frame, under the terrain's own LOD swap). `_checks` entries now carry their ladder (4th element). Flag `entry["fine"]` is set at build time by distance and on crossing in the rescan.
- Nothing deleted (Decision 61: replaced code was the single `point.y - 0.03`; the old check ladder is the same code path, now parametrised).

**Tests (NOT RUN):** `src/worldgen/tools/test_grass_slope_lie.gd` - `godot --headless --path . --script res://src/worldgen/tools/test_grass_slope_lie.gd`. On a 37 degree plane of collision, at 4x4, 3x3, 2x2 and 1 samples per cell: card heights from `lie_y` match a ray at the card's own xz within 5 cm; the old flat height is >0.5 m off in the 1-sample band (shows the symptom); far and near samples give the same card height at the same xz; and a built cell within 64 m gets the fine ladder while a cell at 100 m does not. It fails without the fix (no `lie_y`). Not covered headless: real LOD 1/2 vs LOD 0 collision difference (needs the voxel terrain).

**Per-frame cost (UNMEASURED):** `lie_y` is a few float ops per card at build time (the card loop already does ~30 ops per card): negligible. Fine-ground re-check adds up to 2 re-casts x 5 rays per cell when it first enters 64 m; they go through the existing CHECK_CELLS_PER_FRAME 3 / CHECK_USEC 300 us cap, so bounded per frame; at a walk about 13 cells/s cross the ring (cap 90 checks/s). Rebuilds it triggers are the existing build path, within RAYS_PER_FRAME/BUILD_USEC. Not measured; GTX 1050 class check needed on the PC (`grass: check ground` and `grass: build` probes in frame_probe).

**Regression risks:** (1) coarse normals that are noisy would shift cards slightly (bounded: |dy| <= 0.5*0.95*spacing*1.7); (2) FINE_GROUND_M 64 relies on the unverified module default lod_distance 48; if the fine level reaches less/more, the second (8 s) check is the safety net; (3) more in-place rebuilds near 64 m (once per cell) - visible only if ground really moved > 0.35 m, and then together with the terrain mesh swap.

**Not done / design questions (recorded decision):** did not change density, view distance or card widths (design). Did not make far cells use analytic `height_at` (terrain mesh at distance is the coarse LOD, so analytic heights would float over the visible mesh). No protocol/save/catalog change.
