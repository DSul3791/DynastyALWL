# The 32 kits (guide F-27), 2026-10-31, Kathleen

Before: one identical box in different tints. Now each kit is a small parcel of what it makes (shape "parcel", family in bits[0], colour tints it): fire = stones and a bundle of sticks, roll = a rolled leaf or thatch roof or wall tied with cord (lean-to, thatch wall, roof, doorway), planks = a tied stack of boards (wall, floor, roof, foundation, doorway with a dark gap, window with a dark opening), bench = a tabletop with its legs bundled beside it (workbench; the rack with pegs, the vice and woodworking table with jaws and a screw, the log workbench), chest = a small chest with strap and lid (woven and plank), dome = a stone or brick dome with an arch and stones (forges by size and tier, the master forge with a gold band and red gems, the oven and clay hearth), pot = a pot on a tripod of sticks, bed = a thatch mat with stakes (rough bed, thatch floor), snare = a bent stick with a twine loop, ladder = two rails and rungs, prop = a post with a cap (timber prop and set), sawmill = a laid-out saw frame with a blade.

References: re-enactor and survival-guide photographs of rolled thatch and reed mats, bundled poles and lashed benches; museum drawings of simple frame saws and bench vices; the guide's item sheets (art/playthrough/sheets) showing the identical boxes.
