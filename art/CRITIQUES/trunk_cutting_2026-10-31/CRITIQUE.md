# Cutting a felled trunk into logs (Dave, 2026-10-31), Kathleen

What changed: chopping a felled trunk no longer gives a log to the pack or the shoulder. After the branch blows, every third blow cuts a log (8 ft, the last log takes whatever is left) off the FAR end of the trunk: it drops on the ground beside the trunk as an ordinary loose log item (`drop_in_world`, so the existing drop cap applies: 64 loose drops within 12 m), the trunk record shortens (`length`, with `orig_length` so the cut end is thicker, `landed` so it is not felled again, `cut_blows`, `logs_left`, all saved), and the client draws the shorter trunk with a fresh pale cut face. Blows only count while the player stands within reach of the next cut line (the middle of the piece that comes off); otherwise the blow does nothing and the player is told "Walk along the trunk to the cut line." A pale band on the trunk marks the next cut line on key woods. Sticks, leaves, bark, seeds and roots still go straight to the pack. The shoulder is never touched by chopping; a full shoulder now says "Put your log down first (Q)."
