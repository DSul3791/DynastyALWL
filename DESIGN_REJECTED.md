# Rejected Ideas

Record of ideas considered and explicitly rejected by the project owner, with reasons where given. Check this before proposing something. If it is here, it has already been turned down, and re-proposing it without new information wastes the project owner's time.

Format: one entry per idea. **What was proposed**, **status** (always [Rejected]), **why** (in the owner's own stated reasoning where available; otherwise "no reason recorded"), and where the current rule lives. Things that were only replaced by a better later version of the same idea (superseded) do not belong here; they stay in the revision history of `DESIGN_IDEAS.md`.

New entries go at the end of their section, with the date.

## Time and calendar

- **72-minute game day.** [Rejected] (2026-10-12) Why: one 60-minute game day gives more day and night cycles per session and encourages short weekday visits to keep farms going, which spreads server load across the week. Current rule: `DESIGN_IDEAS.md` Section 7.5.
- **Calendar years of exactly 7 or 14 real days, and time ratios near 26 or 52.** [Rejected] (2026-10-12) Why: a weekly player would see the same point of the year on every visit. The chosen year of 15.2 real days is not a multiple of 7. Current rule: Section 7.2.

## Characters and death

- **Players dying of disease.** [Rejected] (2026-10-14) Why: dying of disease would be a terrible way to die in a game, and there are enough things trying to kill players already. Player characters get at most a one-game-day flu. NPCs can still fall sick and die. Current rule: Section 8.7.
- **Remains as a tombstone or loot bag.** [Rejected] (2026-10-14) Why: no reason recorded beyond the preference for a body with a backpack and all gear on it that decays over time. Current rule: Section 6.1, Remains.

## Towns and settlements

- **The name "Metropolis" for a top settlement stage.** [Rejected] (2026-10-14) Why: the owner is not a fan of the word and prefers "settlement". Current rule: Section 8.17.
- **Towns shrinking to a lower stage.** [Rejected] (2026-10-14) Why: stated by the owner: towns do not shrink; a town stays a town or a city but falls into ruin. Stages never go down. Current rule: Section 8.17.

## Resources

- **Aluminum as a resource.** [Rejected] Why: it was unknown in medieval times (first isolated in the 1820s). Current rule: Section 5 resource list.
