# Three plans toward functioning item build orders

Date 2026-10-31. Written by the playthrough session. The numbers come from `PLAYTHROUGH_BUDGET.md` (generated by `art/playthrough/build_orders.py` from the recipe data and stated gathering parameters) and `PLAYTHROUGH_STATUS.md` (generated by `status_check.py`).

## Where the build orders stand

The milestone table (raw cost and hands-on minutes for each step, from first stone axe to stone forge) lives only in `PLAYTHROUGH_BUDGET.md`, which is generated from the recipe data; it was copied here on 31 Oct and removed on 1 Nov so the two cannot disagree. The numbers below are the supply side.

Supply: within 100 m of the spawn the flora placer puts 142 nettles, 214 tall grass, 33 reeds and 577 ferns, against a whole early order of 34 fibre (about 23 nettles); the thatch house needs about 71 tall grass plants. The fast part of the game has the supply; the slow part is not the gathering but the missing items (F-16, F-02, F-23) and the learn system.

## Where progression is too slow or too fast

| Verdict | Where | Why (numbers) |
|---|---|---|
| **Too fast** | fire, axe, lean-to, bed | the first four milestones cost under 3 minutes of hands-on time together, with no discovery step (Vintage Story gates tools behind knapping flint; Valheim gates them behind several trees) |
| **Fine** | workbench, hammer, spear, knife, pickaxe | each under 1 minute, but they unlock real choices (the bench level, the hammer tier) |
| **Too slow for what it gives** | thatch house | 144 fibre, 285 thatch, 84 sticks for one room that gives only cover |
| **Impossible (not slow)** | wooden room, metal, leather | F-16, F-23, F-02 |
| **Unbalanced** | stone forge | needs 2 hides (a rabbit kill each), 36 stones and 10 clay, but nothing can be smelted yet |

## Plan 1: acceptance bands for the first hours (a CI check on pace)

*Goal:* a tuning change cannot silently make the first hour 5 times slower or free.

* Dave sets target bands for the first-time player (hands-on, times the 3x first-time factor built into the script): axe by minute 5, fire by 8, shelter by 15, bed and workbench by 25, spear and knife by 30; thatch house inside two game days; forge inside a game week.
* Add `--check` to `art/playthrough/build_orders.py`: exit 1 when a milestone leaves its band (raw materials from the data, seconds from the parameter table).
* Wire it into `tools/run_tests.sh` as a fast step (no Godot needed, under a second).
* Owner: Tyler (wiring); Dave (the bands). Size: small.
* Proof: today's table passes except where a chain is dead (the check must also fail when an ingredient has no source: the audit already knows).

## Plan 2: a test that walks the chain from an empty pack

*Goal:* a failure like F-16 (a dead log recipe) is caught by the build, not by a playthrough.

* Extend `src/worldgen/tools/test_first_hour.gd` (job 78) into `test_progression_chain.gd`: start with nothing; take only what gather yields (drops.json minimums) and recipes give; reach, in order: axe, fire lit, lean-to, bed, workbench, tool rack, stone forge, a chopping block from a real felled `log_<species>`, lumber by the wedge, a wall section, a lit forge.
* Each milestone asserts and prints one line; the test fails on the first dead step and names the missing ingredient (the audit script already computes this).
* Uses the real `server_items.gd` like `probe_playthrough.gd`; about 30 seconds headless.
* Owner: Tyler. Size: medium. Depends on F-16 for the later steps; the early ones can ship now.

## Plan 3: supply budget for every starting biome

*Goal:* the build order must be achievable wherever a player spawns, not only in the temperate forest.

* Generalise `probe_spawn.gd` to take any spawn point; run it for the world's starter biomes (temperate forest, grassland, marsh, heath, coast, boreal).
* For each, compare the supply in 100 / 200 / 400 m with the demand rows of `PLAYTHROUGH_BUDGET.md` (fibre, thatch, bedding, sticks by trees, stones by scatter, clay by wet soil, hide by animals) and flag the tier where the biome starves (example: no tall grass or reeds in a pine forest means no thatch beyond bark).
* Output a table in `PLAYTHROUGH_BUDGET.md` and a one-line verdict per biome.
* Owner: flora session (the density tools exist: `flora_density.gd`), me for the demand side. Size: medium.
