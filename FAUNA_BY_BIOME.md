## Shore and sea (new)

## Notes
- Food chain balance: prey outnumber predators about 4 to 1; apex animals (bear, eagle) are rarity 4 to 5.
- Arrows and leather: feathers from birds (pheasant, goose, gull, eagle), hide from rabbit, hare, deer, seal; sinew from deer, boar, bear. The first arrows now need no feather (Fibre arrow).
