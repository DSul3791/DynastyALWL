# Controls

The single complete list of every input (keyboard, mouse, held versus tap, context prompts). Dave's rule (2026-10-20): any change that adds or changes an input updates this file in the same push. `python3 tools/check_controls.py` fails if a key or mouse button bound in code is missing from the first column of the tables below; run it before every push.

## Now (terrain client, build 23)

## Swimming (build 377)
In deep water (over about 1.3 m) you float at the surface and swim at 2 m/s (Shift: 3 m/s, tires you faster). Swimming forward (W) follows where the camera looks (BUG-028, Dave 2026-11-04): look up to rise, look down to dive (a level look, within about 5 degrees, swims level; S backs the other way; you never rise above the float line). Space still swims up and Ctrl still dives, whatever the look. Walking into a bank or ice edge less than about 1.1 m above the water pulls you out. Wading slows you in water over the knees. Under a floe or ice sheet you drift to open water; frozen in with no gap you come up through the ice. Swimming counts as work: the server prices it from your position.
Air (BUG-029, Dave 2026-11-04): with the head under water the air runs down (60 s at swimming pace). At zero you are not lifted to the surface and your health is not drained: suffocation races your heart and closes your vision for 15 seconds, then you faint, and fainting with your head still under water is drowning (you die). Swim up and breathe before you faint and it all goes away at once. The exertion collapse on land still never kills (DESIGN_IDEAS 6.16).

## Filling ground (build 378)
With a shovel in hand, right click fills the ground under the crosshair (one shovelful of soil, sand, gravel, clay or stone from the pack per fill, 0.9 m round, within 4.5 m). Fills are saved like digs and survive restarts and re-bakes; water rises or stops at them.
